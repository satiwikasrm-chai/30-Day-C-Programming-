# Eco Action Note - Cafeteria No-Idling Week

**Data note:** The concern below is based on clearly labelled simulated EcoGuard classroom readings, not real campus measurements.

## Issue observed

The simulated seven-day readings for the Cafeteria show occasional AQI values above 150 around busy meal times. Vehicle idling near the entrance is a likely controllable source of pollution exposure for students waiting outside.

## Action proposed

Run a seven-day **No-Idling Week** at the Cafeteria entrance. Place a visible "Switch off engines while waiting" sign at the drop-off area. Student volunteers will remind drivers during lunch hours, and the facilities team will keep the pickup area clear so vehicles do not queue unnecessarily.

## Measurable seven-day target

For the next seven days, record AQI at the Cafeteria at the same lunch-time window each day and count idling vehicles for ten minutes. The target is to reduce the number of observed idling vehicles by **50%** from the first-day baseline and have **no more than one AQI reading above 150** during the week.

## How impact will be checked

The EcoGuard program will store the daily AQI readings and show risk days. A simple paper tally will record idling vehicles. At the end of the week, compare the day-one vehicle count and AQI with the seven-day total. If the target is not reached, move the sign closer to the entrance and repeat the awareness reminder during the next week.
